# Final search quality control

## Known item validation

The final PubMed strategy retrieved all 15 reports in the expanded known-item set by PMID and DOI. The seed set was assembled from prior reviews, domain knowledge and recent discovery.

Known-item recall supports the sensitivity of the strategy but does not prove that every eligible study was retrieved. The seed set over-represented English-language association-football reports.

## Structural checks

The search used the operational three-block structure of outcome, exposure or state, and sport. Direct jump-wording variants, spelling variants and relevant monitoring and recovery terminology were retained. Database-specific syntax was used for PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, Embase via Ovid and SPORTDiscus via EBSCOhost.

## Independent review status

An independent PRESS sign-off was not completed in the available project record. The database searches are final for this review, but the manuscript must not claim that an independent PRESS review was performed unless a qualified information specialist subsequently completes and signs that assessment.
